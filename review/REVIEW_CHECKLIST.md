# Repository Review Checklist

## Series
- Does the episode order build knowledge progressively?
- Are planned topics missing or duplicated?
- Does every episode define its boundary and bridge?

## Engineering
- Are command, state, feedback and physical effect distinguished?
- Are failure modes and degraded states represented?
- Are examples explicitly illustrative?
- Are maintainability, commissioning and lifecycle included?

## Standards
- Is the correct standards family referenced?
- Is the edition date current as of the review date?
- Is a requirement distinguished from guidance or common practice?
- Are clause-level claims supported by a primary source?

## Bilingual parity
- Same objectives and segment structure?
- Same numerical values and standard editions?
- Same safety and cybersecurity cautions?
- Technical terms translated consistently?

## Audio quality
- Real dialogue rather than alternating essays?
- Yael challenges vague claims?
- Amir gives measurable reasoning and examples?
- Duration plausible without filler?
