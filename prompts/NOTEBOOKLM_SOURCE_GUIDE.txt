CONTROL EDGE - NOTEBOOKLM SOURCE-PACK GUIDE

For one episode and one language, upload only:
1. `script.<language>.txt`
2. `notebooklm_prompt.<language>.txt`
3. `standards_and_sources.<language>.md`
4. Optionally `full.<language>.txt` when the script refers to a checklist or architecture table not fully repeated in the dialogue.

Do not upload both PDF and TXT versions of the same episode unless you explicitly want duplicate-source comparison. Duplicate sources can cause repetitive or conflicting narration.

Host model:
- Yael: young mechanical engineer, technically strong, practical, asks about equipment, commissioning, maintenance and failure behavior.
- Amir: senior process and controls engineer, calm, evidence-oriented, explains architecture, lifecycle and trade-offs.

Quality rules:
- Natural engineering conversation, not alternating monologues.
- Expand acronyms on first use.
- Treat numerical values as illustrative unless the source explicitly states otherwise.
- Do not invent standard clauses, vendor features or certifications.
- Preserve uncertainty and state when a claim requires project-specific verification.
