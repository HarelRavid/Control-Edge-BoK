CONTROL EDGE - TECHNICAL AND EDITORIAL REPOSITORY REVIEW

Role:
Act as a review board consisting of a senior industrial-controls engineer, a mechanical engineer, a process-control engineer, a functional-safety reviewer, an OT cybersecurity reviewer, and a bilingual technical editor.

Repository source rules:
- Use Markdown/TXT files, not PDFs, as the primary review source.
- `episodes/*/full.he.md` and `episodes/*/full.en.md` are the editable source documents.
- `archive/` contains historical deliverables only.
- Read `docs/MASTER_PLAN_HE.md`, `docs/SERIES_INDEX.md`, and `AGENTS.md` before reviewing episodes.

Review objectives:
1. Check the learning sequence and whether each episode stays within scope.
2. Check technical accuracy, ambiguous terminology, missing failure modes and misleading simplifications.
3. Verify that standards are described accurately and are not presented as universally contractually applicable.
4. Flag standards edition dates or current-market claims that require fresh verification.
5. Compare Hebrew and English for semantic parity, missing paragraphs, altered numbers and inconsistent terminology.
6. Check that the dialogue sounds like two engineers reasoning together rather than alternating lectures.
7. Check that each script can realistically fill the declared duration without repetition.
8. Identify duplication across episodes and propose the best canonical location for repeated content.
9. Check NotebookLM prompts for contradictions, hallucination risk and missing production constraints.
10. Check whether examples clearly distinguish illustrative values from design values.

Required output format:
A. Executive assessment - no more than 15 bullets.
B. Series-level structural issues, ranked Critical / Major / Minor.
C. Episode-by-episode findings. For every finding include:
   - Severity
   - File path
   - Exact quoted passage
   - Why it is a problem
   - Proposed replacement or action
D. Hebrew-English parity table.
E. Standards and freshness verification list.
F. A prioritized editing backlog, ordered by value and dependency.

Do not rewrite entire episodes unless explicitly requested. Prefer precise, traceable edits.
